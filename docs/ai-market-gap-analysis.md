# AI Market Gap Analysis

This project can win by joining together capabilities that are still fragmented across the current contractor software market.

## What the market already does well

- `Jobber` is strong on field-service basics: quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payments, CRM, AI tools, and an AI receptionist flow for blue-collar service teams.
- `HOVER` is strong on exterior measurement, 3D models, material visualization, and estimate/proposal generation from captured property geometry.
- `magicplan` is strong on mobile capture, room documentation, photos, forms, sketches, and field-to-office handoff.
- `Procore` is strong on unified project data, enterprise security, and AI agents connected to multiple construction workflows.

## Repeated pain points worth building against

- Pricing and scale friction: smaller contractor tools often become expensive fast as teams grow.
- Shallow analytics: many apps tell the owner what happened, but not why margins, close rate, or crew utilization moved.
- Fragmented capture: measurement, photos, notes, scheduling, CRM, materials, and invoicing often live in separate tools or separate product modules.
- Weak offline and sync reliability: field staff still lose trust when notes, photos, or scope changes lag behind connectivity.
- AI as an add-on instead of a system: several products have AI intake, assistants, or generated estimates, but not deep provenance-aware job intelligence.
- Limited brand control or website embedding quality: public intake often looks bolted on.
- Weak revision memory: quote versioning, change-order logic, and negotiation history are often treated as a side effect rather than first-class data.
- Poor source traceability: when AI suggests scope or pricing, many tools do not explain where the number came from in a way that builds contractor trust.

## Product opening for David's Contracting Suite

The strongest wedge is a unified `capture -> interpret -> price -> sell -> execute -> learn` loop for small-to-mid contractor teams.

That means:

- one system for homeowner intake, contractor field capture, AI scope interpretation, material planning, quote revisions, CRM follow-up, consultation scheduling, and owner analytics
- AI that keeps citations, confidence, and job memory instead of only returning plain text
- AR and guided mobile capture that helps both contractors and homeowners gather better measurements before an on-site visit
- a normalized backend so the product can scale into crews, invoices, asset libraries, scan sessions, and long-term AI retrieval

## Notes on interpretation

- The fragmentation point is an inference from official product positioning. For example, HOVER emphasizes integrations into CRM and estimating tools, and magicplan emphasizes getting field capture into the office workflow. That suggests many teams still combine multiple systems instead of operating from a single native job record.
- The analytics, pricing, sync, and embedding pain points are grounded primarily in the June 2, 2026 TechRadar Jobber review.
